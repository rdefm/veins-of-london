Production

Production tab, collapsed-first

Tap the Defaults panel and any recipe card. Steppers, the cover switch, sales approach and ore filters all respond. Bars are display-only here; in the game the markers drag.

Boundary

  • Defaults is a lighter panel with a red DEFAULTS tag. It collapses to one summary line.
  • Recipes sit below under group headers: Needs attention, Custom, Following defaults.
  • Custom cards get a warm border; following cards are flat and quiet.

Less clutter

  • One stock bar with three markers (Make to, Keep, Sell above) replaces three sliders and the text line.
  • Collapsed card: icon, name, stock, mini bar, status chip.
  • Ore filter is one icon strip. The All / Following / Custom row is dropped because groups already split them.
  • Cover contracts is a switch. Explainer text shows only for the selected approach.

Images

  • Existing recipe icons from assets/combat/icons, on a tile tinted by the ore colour.
  • Corner pip shows stock health: green at or above Keep, amber below, red empty.
  • Recipes using the shared placeholder icon (Near Miss and later) will show it until real art exists.

To review

  • Are three groups right, or one flat list sorted by status?
  • Does the mini bar help, or is the chip enough when collapsed?
  • Keep the ore strip, or drop it?
