Proposal · Combat UI · Move levels

Move Book

Every move levels 1 to 5 and gains real abilities at Lv3 and Lv5, but the game only ever shows "Strike: Lv2 · 50 / 90 XP". The player can't see what a level buys, so they can't plan for it. This proposal adds one reusable component, a level track per move, and puts it in five places.

ProfileMoves
The component · try it, tap a move

One track per move. Stops 3 and 5 are a different shape.

Collapsed, each move is one row: its level, a five-stop mini track and a one-line teaser for the next thing it unlocks. That row answers "what am I working toward?" without a tap.

Expanded, it shows three layers, top to bottom:

1 · The level track

Five stops on a rail. The rail fills to your current level, then fills part of the next gap with XP progress, so the XP bar is the track itself. The per-level stat (damage, chip taken, escape chance) sits under each stop, so the whole curve reads at once and your current value is boxed.

2 · The two unlock cards

Lv3 and Lv5 each get a card: what it does in plain words, then the rule in fight terms. Each card has three states: earned (solid), next (dashed red, with XP to go) and locked (faded).

3 · XP line

Exact XP to the next level and what earns it (10 per use, 4 if it fizzles), so grinding a move in the Gym has a clear target.

Why it looks like this

Design rules

Shape marks the milestones

Lv3 and Lv5 are diamonds and every other stop is a circle. That reads without colour, at mini-track size, and for colour-blind players.

Red means "next", nothing else

The one accent is ui_action_red, used only for the next stop and the next unlock. Calc gold stays reserved for calc and cash (ui-vision §6), so milestones are never gold.

Existing card chrome

Uses the Profile app's existing card chrome, with no new themed prop, so it follows the dark toggle for free.

Effect first, rule second

Each unlock card leads with what changes for you ("block a hit outright") and puts the mechanic underneath in smaller text. That way it works for skimmers and for planners.

Data-driven, no new numbers

Every value on the track and the cards comes from moveLevelEffects in combat_rounds.json. Only the player-facing description strings are new, in data next to the numbers.

Stops show what you have, not a delta

Stops show absolute values (escape 75%), not "+5%". The curve is legible and the boxed value is literally what applies right now.

Decisions

  1. Stun rework. Per-level chance to stun a Guarded target: — / 10 / 20 / 30 / 40% (replaces the flat 80% from Lv3). Lv3 unlock: a landed Stun takes 2 actions instead of 1. Lv5 unchanged: target stays Open for its next 2 actions.
  2. Feint auto-guard in words. A second track row: Chance / Greater / Highest. Percentages stay in data only.
  3. Enemy move levels. Long-press an enemy. Each level follows the same fog roll as reading its moves, so it can show ?. Once the enemy uses a move, its true level shows for the rest of the fight.
  4. Home. The full Move Book lives in the Profile app, with compact rows in Arnie's gym (spar) that open the same sheet. It's removed from the HQ home workout modal.
  5. Level 10. Deferred to the skill-level-cap-10 work.
