# Event rewrite brief

## Tone

- Present-tense, second-person London: money pressure, ordinary objects. Danger stays dangerous.
- Douglas Adams / Terry Pratchett / *The Office* blend: dry observation, practical absurdity, awkward work talk. Humour sits beside tension; no whimsy or camera winks.
- Show the strange thing happening before anyone explains it. Explain calc through what it does, costs, and sells for, in words a newcomer would use.

## Characters
- Archie is blunt, funny and protective through actions rather than speeches. A bit cockney, a salesman through-and-through. 
- The player notices risk, asks sensible questions, and makes a choice for a concrete reason.

## Card craft

- One clear beat per short, mobile-readable card. Alternate action, reaction and dialogue; let pauses do work. Labels mark time/place or a turn in tension.
- Keep each speaker distinct. Avoid exposition disguised as dialogue, ornate metaphors, and narration that tells the player how to feel.
- Make cause and effect legible: why the player is present, what goes wrong, how they escape, why they agree to the next step. Check the neighbouring event for the handoff.

## Proposal format and boundaries

- Deliver a Markdown file in this folder with a brief intent note and a complete, valid `json` fenced block using the event's existing schema.
- Preserve `id`, `on_complete`, canonical names, and existing image order unless a proposed change is called out explicitly. Keep story changes consistent with `docs/REFERENCE.md`, `CONTEXT.md`, and adjacent events; the HTML prototype is prose reference only.
- These are prose drafts. Leave `data/events/*.json` untouched until asked to apply one. Flag each new-prose file `PROSE-REVIEW:` in the report.
