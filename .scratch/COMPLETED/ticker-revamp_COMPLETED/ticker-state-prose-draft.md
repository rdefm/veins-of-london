# The Ticker — state article prose draft

Status: Implemented in the game; prose remains for human review.

Scope: headline, deck, and one-paragraph story for all 15 barometer states. Impact figures must come from live effects, not this prose. The Austerity copy assumes the requested +5% weekly living-cost change in ticket 12. Ore Regulation copy assumes its mugging modifier is removed in ticket 13 while its crafted-item demand effect remains. The broader wording in Social Unrest and Crime Wave is editorial; current mechanics remain specific to vein raids and home raids.

## The Economy

### Stable

**Headline:** Economic outlook remains steady as London traders watch daily quotes

**Deck:** The wider economic reading has not changed, though individual prices can still move.

**Story:** London’s economic barometer remained steady today, with no new citywide change in demand for crafted goods. Traders continue to work from the daily market quote, which responds to stock and buying activity.

### Economic Boom

**Headline:** Demand for crafted goods rises across London

**Deck:** A stronger market is bringing more buyers to finished goods.

**Story:** Orders for crafted items are rising across London, giving dealers a larger pool of potential buyers. The increase affects demand throughout the item market. Its effect on any individual quote will depend on available stock and the trading recorded before the next daily close.

### Recession

**Headline:** Recession reduces London demand for crafted goods

**Deck:** Dealers face fewer buyers for finished items.

**Story:** Demand for crafted goods has fallen as the recession takes hold. Dealers carrying finished stock may have to wait longer for buyers, while those still trading ore will continue to face prices set by the day’s supply and demand. The downturn does not impose a fixed reduction on ore quotes.

### Financial Crisis

**Headline:** Be a Lady demand rises during financial crisis

**Deck:** One finished item attracts more buyers as risks on the street increase.

**Story:** Demand for Be a Lady has increased sharply during the financial crisis, setting it apart from the wider item market. At the same time, the risk of mugging has risen across London. Dealers moving valuable stock must weigh that exposure alongside the day’s market quote.

### High Inflation

**Headline:** Weekly living costs rise as item demand increases

**Deck:** Households face higher bills while buyers place more orders for crafted goods.

**Story:** High inflation has increased weekly living costs across London. Demand for crafted items has also risen, adding pressure to a market already shaped by daily changes in stock and trade. The barometer does not set a higher price for every good; households will see the increase directly in their living-cost bill.

## London Life

### Stable

**Headline:** London street activity remains at usual levels

**Deck:** The social barometer records no new citywide shift.

**Story:** Street activity is holding at its usual level, with no additional effect from the social barometer on raids or mugging risk. Individual incidents remain possible. For traders carrying calc or tending veins, the stable reading leaves ordinary precautions as relevant as they were yesterday.

### Social Unrest

**Headline:** Unrest raises risk of street theft and property crime

**Deck:** Police attention is stretched as disorder spreads between boroughs.

**Story:** Unrest across London has increased the risk of mugging, theft, and breaking and entering. Police attention is being drawn elsewhere, leaving more room for opportunistic crews. The rise in risk applies across the city, although whether a particular trader or property is attacked still depends on its circumstances.

### Lockdown

**Headline:** Lockdown measures add to weekly living costs

**Deck:** Household budgets bear the immediate cost of the new restrictions.

**Story:** Lockdown notices have been issued across London, and weekly living costs have risen while the measures remain in force. Traders are assessing the additional expense alongside their usual market commitments. The current market record does not establish a fixed change to ore prices or a closure of travel routes.

### City Festival

**Headline:** City Festival lifts demand for four crafted items

**Deck:** Blast, Shield, Black Hole and Wormhole draw more buyers.

**Story:** Festival crowds have brought stronger demand for Blast, Shield, Black Hole and Wormhole. The increase concerns those finished goods rather than a guaranteed rise in physics ore prices. Ore quotes will reflect the stock and trading activity recorded by the London market.

### Crime Wave

**Headline:** Crime wave increases mugging and property-raid risks

**Deck:** Traders face greater exposure both on the street and where they keep stock.

**Story:** Mugging risk has risen across London during the crime wave. The chance of a raid on properties has also increased, making the location and amount of carried ore more consequential for its owner. Neither measure records an attack in itself, but both change the risks traders face.

## World News

### Stable

**Headline:** Westminster brings no new political pressure to the trade

**Deck:** The political barometer remains unchanged.

**Story:** No political shift with a new direct effect on London’s calc trade has been recorded. Market demand, living costs and street risk can still change under the economic and social conditions in force. For now, the political reading adds no further pressure of its own.

### Conflict Abroad

**Headline:** Conflict abroad lifts demand for combat supplies

**Deck:** Buyers seek Shield, Blast, Healing Burst and Black Hole.

**Story:** Demand has risen for four finished items used in combat: Shield, Blast, Healing Burst and Black Hole. The change does not set a direct price for the ores used to make them, though shortages and subsequent trade may affect market quotes over time. Mugging risk has also increased during the conflict.

### Austerity

**Headline:** Austerity measures raise weekly living costs

**Deck:** Households face a higher bill under the latest round of cuts.

**Story:** Austerity measures are set to increase weekly living costs across London. The higher bill comes alongside a greater risk of mugging, adding pressure on traders who move stock in person. The change affects household costs and street risk; it does not set the market price of the goods they sell.

### Ore Regulation

**Headline:** Ore regulation increases demand for crafted goods

**Deck:** New rules put pressure on the item trade without setting a fixed price.

**Story:** The government has announced tighter controls on the ore trade. Demand for crafted goods has risen under the new regulatory climate, although the effect on individual quotes will depend on stock and trading. Dealers are reviewing the rules as they decide what to carry and when to sell.

### Election Period

**Headline:** Election period softens changes in item demand

**Deck:** Campaign activity reduces the force of demand shifts from other conditions.

**Story:** The election period is muting changes to crafted-item demand caused by the other active barometer readings. Buyers and sellers will see a smaller demand shift than those conditions would produce on their own.
